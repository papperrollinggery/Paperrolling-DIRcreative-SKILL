# Photography co-design investigation

## Observed problem and attribution

A supplied private production case had strong original environment images:
dark architectural foregrounds, layered courts, water routes, distant observers
and controlled warm/cool separation. Its first narrative handoff emphasized
faces and the exchanged object in a 65mm medium-close view. Those facts were
readable, but the setting's depth and narrative opportunities became secondary.
Later environment-led shots were repairs and are not evidence of the initial
design process. The private source images and local activity records are not
part of this package.

This evidence supports a planning/handoff issue, not a claim that the image
model lost rendering ability. The compiled specification retained the authored
camera choice. Nor does the evidence establish that every creative role was
actually invoked in that task. A photographed close-up is valid; the problem
is allowing a sequence's useful spatial and photographic decisions to occur
only after its shots have been fixed.

The pre-change policy gave Jingzao frame-level visual authority at
`asset_compile` and `frame_compile`, with the narrative handoff consuming locked
shots and camera/tension decisions. Its installed methods already explain
viewer position, depth roles, camera/subject distance, narrative framing and
readability. These methods can improve conception before compilation rather
than merely restate an overconstrained shot packet.

## Integration boundary

Use the existing Studio perspectives and gap selector. Photography contributes
to story/blocking design while DIR retains story, canonical state and adopted
shots. Hard source facts and approved decisions remain authoritative; proposed
lens/crop choices remain creative variables. The later image compiler consumes
the resulting choices through its existing contract. No new controller,
generation authorization, reference requirement or mandatory shot-count form is
introduced.

Reference highlights are observable mechanisms with jobs in the actual film:
an aperture conceals a watcher, a water route constrains retreat, an upper level
changes a character's gaze. Retaining a palette or generating a beautiful scene
asset alone does not demonstrate this transfer. In a reference-free scene,
derive the same kinds of relationships from the story and world rules.

## Verification design

Compare reference-backed and reference-free scenarios using the same story
facts, medium, aspect ratio and input bundle per scenario. Compare direct
Jingzao design, an explicitly reconstructed old camera-lock baseline, and
co-designed DIR/Jingzao treatment. Do not prescribe the same lens or crop to all
arms: their choice is the variable under investigation. Preserve exact prompts,
reference order, provider hashes and resulting images outside the source tree.

Review actual images for event/custody fidelity, meaningful environment,
viewer position/depth, visual hierarchy and non-target deterioration. Inspect
a coherent wide/medium/detail plan separately for cut information and spatial
continuity. A single successful still cannot validate a full sequence; a small
unseeded comparison is qualitative evidence, not a causal performance estimate.

## Results and limits

The fresh natural story-only entry selected `client_story`, retained
`screenwriting-story-craft` as owner and selected Jingzao as an isolated method
collaborator before shot development. Its current body and two photographic
references were resolved and hash-bound; main context was 19,280 bytes and
method context 53,557 bytes, within unchanged budgets. Selection itself remained
`pending_host_read_and_apply`; the primary host separately read the methods and
applied the scale-specific reading correction below. A selector status does not
stand in for that application or for generation.

Six controlled native prompt packages passed the installed provider's real
spec validation, compilation and prompt lint. Six native image calls returned,
followed by two targeted wide-view attempts and two local repair attempts.
The reference-backed calls used the same ordered three-image bundle; the
reference-free calls attached no images. Local repair calls used their own
parent image and are separate from the matched comparison.

The initial co-designed C views did not fully achieve their requested wide
framing. Repeated face/hand-detail demands still competed with the establishing
view, despite a wider lens label. Assigning the whole spatial relationship the
dominant read and reserving fine contact detail for an insert produced visibly
full-body environmental views in both scenarios. The old baseline B retained
some environment and is not evidence that DIR always removes backgrounds.
This is a useful observed correction, not a universal quality or speed claim.

Independent pixel review found factual failures worth retaining: extra people
in one wide courtyard attempt, wrong receiving-hand choices in some ferry
attempts, and unfulfilled precise key-to-palm contact. The local courtyard
repair removed background people while retaining its wide composition. The
ferry repair improved hand separation but did not establish full hand/contact
acceptance. These samples are composition and failure evidence, not a complete
set of approved narrative frames. The two three-shot plans were reviewed for
information progression; continuous generated coverage and moving edits remain
unverified.

Native outputs were approximately 1,930 by 810 pixels, not the specified
2,868 by 1,200. No post-crop was used to simulate exact output conformity.
The compiler's model/quality/size declarations were not supported arguments of
the native image tool and are not claimed as adopted backend settings. Private
images, exact local prompts and tool/source paths remain in the local evidence
folder and are excluded from the public Skill package.

Code review corrected a document-media advisory omission. Fresh-process
regressions cover natural story-only selection, role-scoped video/document
advice, unchanged image compile media, negative requests, missing/oversized
providers, unchanged ordinary asset references and no unauthorized execution.
The Skill Stack audit passed 67 positive, 67 negative and 34 realistic cases;
the related routing/context/discovery tests and full source validation passed.
