# Product CG expression research: transferable methods

**Research date:** 2026-09-08  
**Scope:** public, official case pages and one official technology-partner production breakdown.  
**Evidence rule:** `author_description` records what the studio or official partner says. `still_observation` records what is visible in the supplied official page stills; where a still has not been independently inspected in this pass, it is marked as pending. `inferred_transfer` is an analysis for DIR product CG work. `unverified_motion` records what cannot be claimed from page text or unviewed video. No video timecodes are inferred.

The pages below include official stills and, in several cases, an embedded Vimeo player. They are suitable for later visual review without downloading or redistributing source media.

## Cases

### 1. Vizio — 4K HDR Films / FutureDeluxe

**Official source:** [FutureDeluxe case page](https://futuredeluxe.com/work/vizio)

**Category:** consumer electronics / display technology

**author_description (source fact):** FutureDeluxe describes more than twelve live-action and CG abstract shorts made to communicate Vizio HDR televisions’ colour, clarity, contrast, 4K detail and cinematic qualities. The case is framed around making display performance vivid and attention-grabbing rather than explaining the television as an object.

**still_observation:** The official page supplies a large set of stills under two tonal descriptions, “cinematic/dramatic/bright” and “bright/fun/colourful/everyday wonder.” A fresh frame-by-frame inspection is still required before treating those as a shot grammar.

**inferred_transfer:** Start from the display fact, then design abstract colour, luminance and contrast behaviour that lets the audience feel the claimed viewing difference before the product beauty shot. The abstraction should remain tied to a measurable or demonstrable screen attribute.

**unverified_motion:** The page text does not establish the order, duration, camera path or transition logic of the shorts. Abstract colour movement is not evidence that a television has a particular measured gamut, brightness or HDR performance.

### 2. Converse CX / FutureDeluxe

**Official source:** [FutureDeluxe case page](https://futuredeluxe.com/work/converse-4)

**Category:** footwear / foam and textile behaviour

**author_description (source fact):** The studio says the CX Foam shoe was communicated through three product features: Stretch, Movement and Flexibility. It produced a product film and key visuals intended to show the personality of the CX version of the Chuck Taylor All Star.

**still_observation:** The main official hero inspected by the main thread shows a clean white shoe paired with a translucent, inflated, bending tube-like form. The shoe remains the recognition anchor while the soft transparent form supplies a visual analogue for flexibility. This is an observation of a supplied hero still, not a claim about every frame.

**inferred_transfer:** Translate each footwear claim into one controlled material action: stretch, bend, compress, recover or change contact. Keep the shoe’s silhouette, sole, upper and contact with the floor readable while the proxy material demonstrates the intended behaviour.

**unverified_motion:** The public page does not provide timecodes or prove that the tube performs a one-to-one simulation of CX Foam. A flexible-looking proxy cannot establish cushioning, durability, comfort or laboratory performance.

## Executable research method for a new product CG shot

Use the following chain before writing a prompt or selecting a reference. Record unknowns instead of filling them with visual clichés.

1. **Known facts / unknowns** — List confirmed product geometry, materials, mechanism, function, brand signals and delivery constraints. Mark every unverified property as unknown.
2. **Audience result** — State what the viewer must understand or feel in one sentence. Examples: “the upper flexes without losing the shoe’s identity” or “the printer repeats a precise layered process.”
3. **Material behaviour and contact** — Define the material action, what touches what, what deforms, what returns, and what remains rigid. Add the optical result: reflection, refraction, absorption, translucency, dust, liquid, fibre or particulate response.
4. **Shot information gain** — For each shot or still state the new information it adds: silhouette, feature, mechanism, material, scale, use or emotional association. Remove variants that add no new information.
5. **接镜锚点 / next-shot anchor** — Specify the object, axis, orientation, contact point, colour or material feature that the next image must inherit. A new visual metaphor cannot silently reset product identity or space.
6. **速度曲线 / dwell** — Describe where motion accelerates, decelerates, pauses or holds for recognition. Do not infer exact durations until the actual edit or generation output is available.
7. **研发观察 → 节奏** — If the product has a real production, use or performance context, observe it before inventing rhythm. A production line, pit stop, assembly cycle or repeated contact can supply the tension/release pattern; the observation is a design reference, not proof that the final animation reproduces the real system.
8. **Verification** — Check the result against the known facts, target audience result, material behaviour, information gain and next-shot anchor. Separate visual plausibility from engineering proof and from user acceptance.

The three added cases extend the method in three directions: a product material can become a landscape only when its behaviour remains recognisable; production and performance observation can supply an accountable speed curve; and product-derived typography can be studied as a shape relationship while the source brand’s wording and identity remain non-transferable.

An anonymous local reference consisted of 11 user-provided dry-powder product storyboard images. The useful abstraction is a purple-white studio, reflective dark shell, three-colour pressed powder, and a progression of array → hero product → texture macro → contact/lift → radial powder → powder returning inward to the pan → final hero.

These 11 images describe action states and alternatives; they do **not** automatically represent 11 new shots. A fixed duration must not expand merely because more reference states exist. ## Cross-category limits

- An abstract material metaphor can clarify a product truth, but it is not engineering, cosmetic, sustainability, comfort or performance evidence.
- Brand motifs, heritage references, colours and signature forms remain owned by the source brand; transfer the reasoning pattern, not the recognizable asset.
- A still observation supports composition and material reading only. It does not establish motion, timing, continuity or edit rhythm.
- A production breakdown documents an approach and selected tests; it does not guarantee the same method will work for a different product, renderer or generation model.
- Family-level stylistic consistency requires visual review of the actual generated set. Similar prompts, static assets or structure checks do not prove that every member is visually validated.
