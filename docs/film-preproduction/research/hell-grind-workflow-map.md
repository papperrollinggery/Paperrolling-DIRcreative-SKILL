## Character asset invariant

For new recurring clothed-human work, one headed master image is the default
identity/body/wardrobe output. Separate approved identity and wardrobe/body
inputs remain generation-only provenance. The full operational rule is owned by
`skills/dircreative/references/character-master-sheet.md`; this research map does
not duplicate its layout, scale, headless, detail or visual-review thresholds.

## Matrix boundary

Do not add another all-in-one controller. DIR selects one craft owner,
non-overlapping specialist help, at most one validator, and an execution adapter
only for an authorized side effect. Similar names are not duplicates when they
own different stages: Seedance method, emotion performance, deterministic slot
compilation, preflight validation, output review, and retry diagnosis remain
separate. Physical source Skills are never merged; only DIR routing and
responsibility precedence are consolidated.
