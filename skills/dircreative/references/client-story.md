# Client Story Craft

Use for a client-readable story, concept or treatment whose requested scope
stops before technical shots and asset production. Return the full story,
including its ending. A first concept is only a milestone when more was requested.

## Create and review

Use the supplied audience, duration, product/service facts, characters and tone.
Label strategic assumptions; do not invent product capabilities or research.
Choose an audience change, one core action and a visual or sound idea that makes
this film specific. Let the product or service affect an action, choice or
relationship rather than appearing as an inserted claim.

For causal drama, make pressure, resistance, choice and consequence screenable.
For lyrical or observational films, develop perception, rhythm and relationship
without forcing a secret, crisis or reversal. Keep the user's exclusions.
The ending needs a visible or audible completion of the opening proposition.

Read the actual draft for source fidelity, character voice, natural spoken
rhythm and performance capacity. Preserve project names, coined terms, imagery
and genre register. Original writing needs no author corpus or mandatory
humanization cascade. Revise a demonstrated weakness in the same pass.

## Groups and output

Creative-group judgment covers audience tension, story mechanism and brand role.
Director-group judgment covers performance, staging, camera, sound and edit
feasibility. Named groups can be integrated professional perspectives; real
joint/parallel delegation follows `docs/film-preproduction/director-room-routing.md`.
Do not invent role conversations or a fixed number of alternatives.

Write connected readable paragraphs with enough action and dialogue to convey
the complete film. Give alternatives only when requested or a remaining material
choice belongs to the user. Do not append shot tables, asset matrices or provider
receipts. Continue already authorized work; do not ask for story approval merely
to finish the requested story. Additional craft references are needed only for
a specific unresolved problem, not as another routine writing pass.
