# Studio Development Route Card

Reuse the brief and artifacts; start at the needed film stage.

## Budget

- One controller; zero Threads by default.
- One task reference, at most three useful perspectives and one critical pass.
- Zero routing/audit calls before an obvious first artifact; no full-project
  validation. At least 70% of the answer is usable film content or craft judgment.
- For photography during conception and actual joint dispatch, follow
  `docs/film-preproduction/director-room-routing.md`.

## Execution

1. Reuse the brief and return the requested layer: `client_story`,
   `narrative_storyboard`, `frame_content_spec`, `technical_production` or full
   preproduction. Preserve actual SUPER, UI/data and client claims; do not add a
   matrix to a story-only request.
2. Build one causal spine: tension, brand role where applicable, action,
   start/end state, physical/media rules, continuity, photography and sound.
   Produce a recommended direction and usable first-round artifact after mode selection.
   Give the first completed story or shot artifact as concise content plus an
   openable file, not a process report.
3. For technical scope, use canonical IDs and continuous timecode; derive
   recurring identities, products/props, scenes, shots and generation units.
   Include every scene image and complete director storyboard coverage of
   every shot and required phase: individual frames or a reviewed
   `annotated_reference` strategy, plus required clean model-input frames.
   Mark a representative sample as incomplete for whole-film coverage. Do not
   delay a ready root asset for unrelated downstream coverage.
4. In discussion, answer naturally; only a real direction or production choice
   waits. In direct mode, continue authorized work without stage approvals.
   Existing mode/control behavior is owned by `chat-co-creation-interface.md`.
5. Batch independent assets, preview each saved candidate, and review before a
   real dependent use. Preserve five-view, parent locks and actual QA. The
   pre-video gate and bound coverage sidecar remain required for whole-film or
   sequence completion; a sample or plan remains incomplete.

For `spatial_discussion`, use its task reference and current host display
contract. Keep viewing presentation-only and finish the requested scope.

Return usable content and material limitations; keep internal routing details out.
