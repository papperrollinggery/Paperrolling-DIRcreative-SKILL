## Producer

This is best scoped as a 15-second cinematic short test.

Reason:

- One protagonist.
- One location.
- One high-value prop.
- One clear action.
- Enough tension for 5 shots.
- Small enough to test reference image continuity.

Do not expand into a full short film yet.

## Creative Director

Core concept:

> A courier realizes the package is not just being delivered to the alley; the alley is waiting for the package.

Emotional hook:

- pressure before entry,
- dread inside the alley,
- object reveal,
- unresolved danger.

Visual hook:

- red courier jacket against blue-black rain,
- black confidential delivery bag,
- flickering sign reading `24小时`,
- wet pavement reflecting red neon,
- package seal glowing only when near the alley door.

## Director

Directorial intent:

The sequence should feel like the moment before a larger story begins.

Performance:

- Courier is tired, focused, and afraid but professional.
- He does not run.
- His hesitation sells the danger.

Blocking:

- Starts at alley mouth.
- Moves from left foreground toward center depth.
- Stops near glowing door/intercom.
- Hand tightens on delivery bag before reveal.

## Screenwriter

Candidate logline:

> In a neon rainstorm, a courier enters a forbidden alley to deliver a sealed package, only to discover the package activates the place he was warned to avoid.

No dialogue needed for first test.

Possible voiceover if needed:

> "Some addresses are not meant to be found."

For visual-only MVP, use no VO.

## Cinematographer

Lens and camera strategy:

- 24mm wide establishing shot for alley geography.
- 35mm tracking medium shot behind courier.
- 85-100mm macro insert for package seal.
- 50mm close-up for reaction.
- Slow dolly or locked-off final shot for suspense.

Lighting:

- red sign glow from left,
- cold cyan sign from right,
- rain reflections on ground,
- practical door light at destination.

## Production Designer

Character locks:

- red waterproof courier jacket,
- black utility pants,
- black delivery bag,
- wet hair/face,
- small courier ID patch.

Environment locks:

- narrow alley,
- wet pavement,
- pipes and service doors,
- red courier sign,
- blue `24小时` sign,
- sealed metal door/intercom.

Prop locks:

- black confidential package or bag,
- glowing scanner seal,
- handheld delivery terminal.

## Editor

Recommended duration:

15 seconds, 5 shots.

Pacing:

```text
0-3s establish threat
3-6s approach
6-8s prop reveal
8-12s reaction and hesitation
12-15s final door activation
```

No hard montage. The tension comes from controlled pacing.

## Sound Designer

Default audio policy:

- rain ambience,
- distant traffic hum,
- neon electrical buzz,
- one scanner beep at package reveal,
- no dialogue,
- no music for first visual continuity test.

If adapted for ad/short video, add low synth pulse or VO.

## Model Prompt Engineer

Reference pack implications:

- Character board: courier identity and wardrobe.
- Environment board: alley geography and camera path.
- Prop board: delivery bag/package/scanner seal.
- Storyboard board: 5-shot sequence.

Video adapter warning:

The storyboard board must not be animated as a board.

## Continuity QA

Required locks:

- same courier face and red jacket,
- same black bag/package,
- same alley geography,
- same rain/night lighting,
- scanner glow only appears after package close-up.

Missing decisions:

- whether package is a bag, box, or both,
- exact final reveal,
- whether video output includes sound.

Decision for MVP:

- use black delivery bag containing sealed package,
- final reveal is door activation,
- silent visual-first with optional ambience notes.
