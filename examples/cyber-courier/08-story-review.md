## Review By Role

### Producer

The scope remains executable. It uses one protagonist, one location, one prop system, one story turn, and no dialogue. This is appropriate for the first end-to-end fixture.

Risk: the concept can become too abstract if the door activation is not visually tied to the package glow.

Decision: preserve the red scanner glow as the causal bridge.

### Creative Director

The visual hook is clear: red courier against blue-black rain, black bag, red seal, responsive alley. The story promise points to a larger hidden network without requiring exposition.

Risk: "alley is alive" could become fantasy if later visuals overdo organic movement.

Decision: treat the alley as a dormant machine or address system, not a monster.

### Director

The treatment gives a playable emotional transition: duty, hesitation, recognition, dread, irreversible activation. It can be directed through body tension and gaze rather than dialogue.

Risk: a video model may add running or panic if the prompt says "danger" too broadly later.

Decision: later shot and prompt language should specify controlled movement and restrained fear.

### Screenwriter

The story is structurally complete for 15 seconds:

```text
threshold -> commitment -> object reaction -> recognition -> activation
```

No dialogue is needed. Adding voiceover would reduce the precision of the visual test.

### Editor

The 5-beat structure fits the target duration. The last beat should have enough time to read the door activation. Avoid compressing beat 4 into beat 5 too aggressively.

### Sound Designer

The visual-first policy is sound. Optional later sound cues are clear and minimal: rain, traffic hum, neon buzz, scanner beep. No music is required for MVP.

### Continuity QA

Required continuity locks for Phase C and D:

- same courier identity,
- red waterproof jacket,
- black delivery bag,
- sealed package inside bag,
- red scanner glow only after beat 3,
- same alley geography,
- red sign left,
- blue `24小时` sign deeper/right,
- metal door at far end,
- no added characters.

## Open Questions

None block Phase C.

Deferred decisions:

- exact courier age and face design,
- exact package shape,
- whether ambience is generated by video model or handled in post,
- final door panel design.

These belong to script breakdown, visual bible, and model adapter phases.

## Revision Notes

- Keep the package as a black delivery bag containing a sealed object; do not choose the final prop silhouette until script breakdown.
- Keep the final reveal as door activation; do not reveal a recipient.
- Keep the story silent in Phase C unless a later channel variant requires voiceover.
