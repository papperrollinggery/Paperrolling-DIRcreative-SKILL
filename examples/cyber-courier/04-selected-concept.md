## Logline

In a neon rainstorm, a courier enters a forbidden alley to deliver a sealed package, only to discover the alley itself recognizes and activates the package.

## Theme

Professional duty crossing into unknown systems.

## Story Promise

The viewer should feel that this delivery is the first visible piece of a larger secret network.

## Visual Hook

A red-jacket courier moves through a blue-black rain alley while the sealed package emits a faint red scanner glow that matches the alley signage.

## Beat Structure

| Beat | Time | Function | Visible action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0-3s | Establish forbidden location | Wide shot of courier at alley mouth in heavy rain |
| 2 | 3-6s | Commit to action | Courier steps into alley, gripping black delivery bag |
| 3 | 6-8s | Object reveal | Macro insert of scanner seal glowing on package |
| 4 | 8-12s | Emotional hesitation | Close-up of courier reacting to unseen activation |
| 5 | 12-15s | Suspense hook | Metal door/intercom lights up as courier stops |

## Production Locks

Character:

- same young adult courier,
- red waterproof jacket,
- black utility pants,
- black delivery bag,
- wet face and hair,
- anxious but controlled expression.

Environment:

- same narrow alley,
- red sign on left,
- blue `24小时` sign on right/depth,
- wet reflective ground,
- metal destination door at far end,
- rain, steam, pipes, service panels.

Prop:

- black delivery bag,
- sealed confidential package inside,
- glowing scanner seal,
- handheld terminal if needed.

Sound:

- default visual-first,
- optional rain ambience,
- optional neon buzz,
- optional scanner beep at beat 3,
- no dialogue for MVP.
