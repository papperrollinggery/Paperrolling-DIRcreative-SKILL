# Cyber Courier Idea Intake

## Raw Idea

雨夜，一个赛博快递员必须把机密包裹送到一条没人敢进去的巷子。

## Parsed Intent

- Core subject: cyberpunk courier
- Core situation: urgent delivery under threat
- Core object: confidential package
- Core location: dangerous rain-soaked alley
- Core mood: tension, secrecy, pressure
- Likely format: short film, ad-style cinematic test, or AI video benchmark

## Missing Decisions

- Target channel: film short, ad, short drama, short video, or MV.
- Target duration: 5s, 10s, 15s, 30s, or longer.
- Aspect ratio: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, or cinematic wide.
- Audio policy: silent, dialogue, voiceover, SFX, music, or mixed.
- Product/brand role: pure story or product-like courier service concept.
- Ending: successful delivery, ambush, reveal, or cliffhanger.

## Initial Recommendation

Use this as a 15-second cinematic short test.

Reason:

- The idea has one clear protagonist, one object, one location, and one urgent action.
- 15 seconds is long enough for 5 shots and short enough for current video models.
- It can test character continuity, prop continuity, environment blocking, camera motion, and lighting.
