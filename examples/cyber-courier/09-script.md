## Script

### EXT. SERVICE ALLEY MOUTH - NIGHT - RAIN

Heavy rain turns the street into broken neon. A YOUNG COURIER in a red waterproof jacket stands at the entrance of a narrow service alley, black delivery bag held close against his ribs.

Behind him, the street still moves. Ahead, the alley is almost still: wet pavement, exposed pipes, closed service doors, a red sign on the left, a blue `24小时` sign glowing deeper inside.

The courier checks the address on a small delivery terminal. Correct. His thumb pauses over the confirmation screen. He looks back once toward the street.

He steps into the alley.

Rain hits the hood and shoulders of his jacket. His pace is controlled, not fast. His hand tightens around the delivery bag strap as red neon flickers across the water under his boots.

Inside the bag, something answers.

A sealed matte-black package, visible through the half-open bag, emits a thin red scanner pulse. The pulse matches the red sign behind him.

The courier stops.

He looks down at the package, then up toward the far end of the alley. His jaw tightens. His breathing is visible in the cold rain. He does not run.

At the end of the alley, a metal service door wakes. A narrow light appears around the intercom panel. The panel flickers once, then holds.

The courier stands short of the door, bag still in hand. The red scanner glow reflects in the puddle at his feet.

Cut before the door opens.

## Script Notes

- The courier never says what the package is.
- The alley never moves like a creature; it activates like a hidden machine.
- The red scanner glow is the causal bridge between package and destination.
- The final cut happens before any recipient appears.
- This script is ready for script breakdown and shot design.
