## Title

Wasteland Sanitation Route

## Logline

Before sunrise, a burned-out sanitation worker drives a rusted street sweeper through a zombie quarantine district to collect one glowing waste barrel, accidentally turning city cleanup rules into the perfect survival strategy.

## Core Promise

The apocalypse is terrifying to everyone except the one person still following municipal procedure.

## Story Engine

```text
deadline: dawn
job: collect one glowing barrel
tool: rusted street sweeper
obstacle: zombie crowd
comic rule: the worker refuses to skip paperwork
payoff: the cleanup route saves the district by accident
```

## Visual Hook

Orange sanitation suit against blue-green undead streets; a rattling street sweeper throws sparks, foam, and zombie hands while the hero calmly checks a clipboard.

## Originality Boundary

This is inspired by public benchmark traits around AI zombie shorts, not by the plot, shots, or character design of `Zombie Scavenger`.
