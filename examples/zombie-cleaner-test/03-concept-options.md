## Option A: Wasteland Sanitation Route

A city sanitation worker must collect a glowing biohazard barrel from a zombie quarantine district before sunrise, using a battered street sweeper as both vehicle and weapon.

Strength:

- clear job,
- strong visual object,
- black-comedy contrast,
- easy longform sequence structure.

Risk:

- vehicle continuity must be locked.

## Option B: The Last Recycling Clerk

A bored recycling clerk sorts zombie body parts by municipal category while the undead queue politely outside.

Strength:

- very funny premise,
- strong static location.

Risk:

- less cinematic movement for 180s.

## Option C: Dawn Disinfection Parade

A failed propaganda parade uses cleanup drones, zombie extras, and one nervous worker to stage a fake safe city announcement.

Strength:

- satire and scale.

Risk:

- too many moving subjects for a first E2E fixture.

## Selection

Select Option A.

Reason:

It gives DIRcreative the best test surface: moving vehicle, hero identity, prop objective, zombie crowd, timed route, and clean 15s sequence beats.
