## Audio Policy

No dialogue by default.

Use diegetic sound:

- street sweeper engine,
- warning jingle,
- rotating brushes,
- disinfectant foam,
- wet zombie footsteps,
- Geiger counter,
- compactor belt,
- dawn birds and distant siren fade.

## Script

EXT. QUARANTINE DISTRICT - PRE-DAWN

Blue hazard lights flash over abandoned storefronts. A rusted street sweeper rolls into frame. Its municipal decal reads `SANITATION UNIT 47`.

Inside the cab, the worker checks a clipboard, circles one pickup address, and starts the route.

EXT. FLOODED ALLEY

A glowing waste barrel pulses under a torn quarantine tarp. Zombies turn toward the sound of the sweeper jingle.

The worker lowers the rotating brushes. Sparks and dirty water fly. The zombies shuffle into a strangely neat line.

EXT. SERVICE LANE

The barrel breaks loose and rolls downhill. The worker chases it in the sweeper while zombies follow the glow.

A foam sprayer bursts open, covering the street in white disinfectant. The worker wipes the windshield and keeps driving.

EXT. RECYCLING YARD

The sweeper stalls beside a compactor belt. The worker climbs down, stamps a hazard form on the barrel, then notices the belt waking up.

The zombies follow the glowing barrel onto the belt. The worker hooks the barrel with a broom pole and yanks it clear.

EXT. CLEAN STREET - DAWN

Sunrise hits a washed, quiet street. The worker loads the barrel into the sweeper, clocks out, and receives an automated paper slip:

`OVERTIME DENIED`.

He pockets it, starts the engine, and drives into the next ruined block.
