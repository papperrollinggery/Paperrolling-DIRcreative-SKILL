## Beat Sheet

1. Cold open: a quarantine district wakes under sirens, but one municipal street sweeper starts its night route.
2. The worker checks a clipboard and refuses to abandon the pickup order.
3. A glowing barrel pulses inside a flooded alley.
4. Zombies gather, attracted by the sweeper warning jingle.
5. The worker discovers the broom mechanism pushes zombies into neat rows.
6. A malfunction sprays disinfectant foam and creates a clean corridor.
7. The barrel rolls loose, dragging zombies behind it like parade followers.
8. The vehicle loses power near a recycling compactor.
9. The worker stamps the wrong form and accidentally starts the compactor belt.
10. Zombies shuffle onto the belt while chasing the glow.
11. Dawn breaks; the district is quiet, washed, and absurdly tidy.
12. The worker files the report: route completed, overtime denied.

## Treatment

The film opens on a dead city block where sirens and hazard lights imply disaster. Instead of soldiers, a tired sanitation worker arrives in a rattling municipal street sweeper. He treats the quarantine like a normal night shift.

The objective is one glowing biohazard barrel in a flooded alley. The barrel attracts zombies, but the street sweeper's rotating brushes, warning jingle, and foam sprayers create comic order from chaos. The worker never becomes a superhero. He survives because he understands the machine and the route.

At the midpoint the barrel rolls away and the worker has to steer the crowd through a collapsed service lane. The vehicle loses power near a recycling compactor. In a final bureaucratic joke, he stamps the wrong form, triggering the compactor belt. The zombies follow the glowing barrel onto the belt while the worker drags the barrel clear.

At sunrise the street is clean. The worker clocks out, circles `route completed`, and receives an automated notice denying overtime.

## Channel Fit

Works as a 180s AI short because it has:

- one hero,
- one vehicle,
- one prop objective,
- repeatable location language,
- 12 clear 15s beats,
- limited dialogue,
- strong visual comedy.
