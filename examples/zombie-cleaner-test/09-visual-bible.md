## Visual Identity

Hero:

- tired sanitation worker,
- orange rubber cleanup suit,
- black gloves,
- cracked clear face shield,
- practical posture,
- calm bureaucratic focus.

Vehicle:

- rusted municipal street sweeper,
- oversized rotating front brushes,
- patched foam tank,
- `UNIT 47` decal,
- cracked amber hazard lights.

Prop:

- one glowing green biohazard barrel,
- dented metal,
- warning stripes,
- Geiger pulse,
- never becomes a weapon.

Zombies:

- small crowd, not an army,
- wet municipal workers, office commuters, delivery riders,
- slow awkward motion,
- comic choreography rather than horror gore.

## Palette

```text
sodium amber
pre-dawn blue
sickly green barrel glow
orange suit
wet black asphalt
rusted white vehicle panels
```

## Camera Grammar

- 28mm street and vehicle wides.
- 35mm lateral vehicle tracking.
- 50mm clipboard and worker inserts.
- 85mm gross-comedy macro inserts for brush, barrel, stamp, and foam.
- Top-down sequence references only for planning and video prompt text, not direct first frames.

## Material Rules

- Wet asphalt reflects light but does not become noisy glitter.
- Rubber suit is matte, wrinkled, and worn.
- Vehicle paint is chipped but geometry remains stable.
- Foam is dirty white, not magical smoke.
- Zombies are grimy and comic, not graphic gore.

## Negative Rules

- no superhero armor,
- no military squad,
- no clean sci-fi car,
- no huge horde that breaks model control,
- no excessive gore,
- no fake unreadable microtext,
- no literal storyboard sheet in final video.
